Every kitchen has them: two or three bananas on the counter that went brown a little faster than planned. Don't throw them out. The spottier they are, the better your banana bread will be.

This one is simple. One bowl, no mixer, and about an hour in the oven.

## Ingredients

- 3 very ripe bananas
- 1/3 cup of butter, melted
- 3/4 cup of brown sugar
- 1 egg, beaten
- 1 teaspoon of vanilla
- 1 teaspoon of baking soda
- A pinch of salt
- 1 1/2 cups of all-purpose flour
- A handful of walnuts or chocolate chips, if you like

## Method

1. Heat the oven to 350°F. Butter a 9 x 5 inch loaf pan.
2. Mash the bananas in a large bowl with a fork.
3. Stir in the melted butter, then the brown sugar, the egg and the vanilla.
4. Sprinkle the baking soda and salt over the mix and stir.
5. Add the flour and stir just until you can't see any more dry flour. Fold in the nuts or chocolate chips if you're using them.
6. Pour into the pan and bake for 55 to 65 minutes, until a toothpick in the middle comes out clean.
7. Let it cool in the pan for 10 minutes, then turn it out onto a rack.

It's even better the next day, toasted, with a little butter and a cup of coffee.
