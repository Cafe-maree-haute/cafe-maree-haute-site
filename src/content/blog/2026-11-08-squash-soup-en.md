When the evenings get cooler, this is one of the soups we make most often.

## Ingredients

- 1 butternut squash, peeled and cut in cubes
- 1 onion, chopped
- 2 cloves of garlic
- 4 cups of vegetable stock
- 2 tablespoons of butter
- Salt, pepper and a pinch of nutmeg
- A splash of cream, if you like

## Method

1. Roast the squash at 400°F for about 30 minutes, until soft.
2. Melt the butter in a pot and cook the onion and garlic until soft.
3. Add the squash and the stock. Let it simmer for 15 minutes.
4. Blend until smooth. Season with salt, pepper and nutmeg.
5. Stir in a little cream before serving.
